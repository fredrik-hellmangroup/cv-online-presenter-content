# Published claim provenance

The canonical, owner-approved full public CV source is [`content/sources/master-cv.md`](./content/sources/master-cv.md). Its tracked predecessor, `owner-cv-v1.2.md`, was renamed without changing its content. Owner approval OQ-04 permits public factual content from the current CV, but does **not** approve extra contact details, images or external profile links. The original owner-supplied document fingerprint recorded before this pipeline was `04EFA1B7F6844E628A1F992B9A081025D690CF62CBC3BDFCF4B780FAA02DB03C`; `content/generated-provenance.json` records the SHA-256 of the current canonical source bytes after line-ending normalization.

Generation copies source sections rather than drafting new factual claims. The mapping retains the already-approved page metadata. `content/generated-provenance.json` records source, map, generator and generated-page hashes, plus an integrity hash over the provenance record. Source, map and generator digests use UTF-8 text with line endings normalized to LF; output digests cover the generated UTF-8 bytes. `--check` compares hashes and generated bytes; `--write` refuses to overwrite output that differs from either the initial bootstrap hashes in the mapping or the last generated provenance.

`content/pages/profile.md` is neither read nor generated. Contact and synthetic demo pages remain outside the output allowlist and are never modified by the generator. `content/pages/contact.md` is independently owner-approved as configured in `content/site.json`; it is not content generated from the CV source. `content/pages/demo-profile.md` stays synthetic and draft-only.
